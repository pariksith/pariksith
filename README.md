### ⚖️ [CaseFlow](https://github.com/pariksith/CASEFLOW) — AI Litigation Workflow for Indian Advocates

> *Court-ready legal notices from a client's raw story — in under 10 minutes.*

An opinionated LegalTech platform built specifically for **Section 138 NI Act (Cheque Bounce)** cases in India. Advocates speak or type a client's story; GPT-4o extracts a structured legal timeline; a guided 6-question flow captures case facts; and the system generates a court-ready `.docx` notice — with hardcoded legal sections, automated deadline tracking, and email reminders.

**The design principle:** AI writes language. Humans and hardcoded law handle the rest.

`Next.js 14` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `GPT-4o` `Twilio OTP` `Resend` `Vercel` `i18n (EN + TA)`

🔗 [caseflows.vercel.app](https://caseflows.vercel.app)

### 🏥 [CHEP — Complete Hospital Ecosystem Platform](https://github.com/pariksith/COMPLETE-HOSPITAL-ECOSYSTEM-PLATFORM)

> *One platform. Every hospital workflow.*

A production-grade, full-stack hospital management system built in PHP + MySQL — covering the complete patient journey from registration to discharge. Includes dedicated modules for doctors, receptionists, pharmacists, lab technicians, billing staff, and administrators — plus telemedicine, ICU monitoring, PWA support, AI symptom checker, triage, payroll, and health forecasting.

This isn't a tutorial project. It's a hospital in a repository.

`PHP` `MySQL` `HTML/CSS/JS` `PWA` `Telemedicine` `AI Health Insights` `Pharmacy` `Lab` `Billing`

🔗 [chep.infinityfreeapp.com](https://chep.infinityfreeapp.com/index.php)

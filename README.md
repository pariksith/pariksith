## ⚖️ CaseFlow — AI Litigation Workflow for Indian Advocates

> *Court-ready legal notices from a client's raw story — in under 10 minutes.*

An opinionated LegalTech platform built specifically for **Section 138 NI Act (Cheque Bounce) cases** in India.
Client tells their story (voice or text)
↓
GPT-4o extracts a structured legal timeline
↓
6-question guided flow captures case-specific facts
↓
System generates a court-ready .docx legal notice
→ Hardcoded legal sections (not hallucinated)
→ Automated 30-day deadline tracking
→ Email reminders via Resend
→ OTP auth via Twilio
→ Full Tamil + English i18n

text

**Design principle:** AI writes language. Humans and hardcoded law handle the rest.
Stack → Next.js 14 · TypeScript · Supabase · GPT-4o · Twilio · Resend · Vercel · i18n (EN + TA)
